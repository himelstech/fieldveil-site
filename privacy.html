Privacy Policy

Last updated August 2, 2026

FieldVeil is built by a solo, independent developer. This policy explains what data the app does and does not collect.

Data Collection

FieldVeil does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics or advertising SDKs of any kind.

Hand and Room Tracking

FieldVeil uses Apple's ARKit to read hand gestures and your room's geometry so it can apply visual scenes to your surroundings. This processing happens entirely on-device. None of this data is ever transmitted off your headset, stored by the developer, or shared with any third party.

In-App Purchases

FieldVeil offers optional, voluntary tips via Apple's In-App Purchase system (StoreKit). Payment processing is handled entirely by Apple; the developer never receives your payment details. Tips unlock no additional content or features.

Third Parties

FieldVeil does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

FieldVeil does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it collects no personal data at all.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised date above.
